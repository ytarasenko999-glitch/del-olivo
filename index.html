Picual alone can be fierce; Arbequina alone is delicate and fades quickly. Together they give depth, balance and a long life in the bottle.

Questions

What’s the difference between extra virgin and “olive oil”?

Extra virgin is the top grade: mechanical extraction only, acidity no higher than 0.8%, and no taste defects according to an official tasting panel. A bottle labelled simply “olive oil” is usually refined oil blended with a little virgin oil.

Why does it taste bitter and catch in the throat?

That’s polyphenols — natural compounds in fresh olives. Bitterness and a peppery finish are signs of a good early-harvest oil, not a fault.

Can I cook with it?

Yes — extra virgin is stable when heated. To enjoy its full aroma, though, add it at the end or use it raw.

Why does it turn cloudy in the cold?

The olive’s natural waxes solidify at low temperatures. At room temperature the oil turns clear again — quality is unaffected.

How should I store it?

Somewhere dark at 14–20 °C, tightly closed and away from the stove. Once opened, enjoy within [X] months.
